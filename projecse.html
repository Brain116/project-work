Frequently asked questions

Is Raenest a bank?
Raenest is a Financial Technology (FinTech) Company, not a Bank. Banking services are provided by banking partners. Card services are provided by Raenest's technology partners, authorized by Visa and Mastercard to issue cards operating over their networks.
How long does it take to get my bank account?
Once you complete sign-up and any required identity verification, available account details will appear in your Raenest app. Some accounts may require additional review.
What's the fee for receiving in my bank account?
Fees can depend on the account currency and payment method. Check the fee details in your Raenest app before completing a transfer.
What is the Raenest USD Visa Card and how is it related to Raenest?
The Raenest USD Visa Card is a payment card available through the Raenest platform. The card is issued by a licensed card partner; Raenest provides the platform to access and manage it.
Can I use my USD account on Upwork, Deel, Fiverr, or Payoneer?
You can add your Raenest USD account details to supported platforms for receiving payments. Check each platform's requirements and accepted account types before adding your details.
